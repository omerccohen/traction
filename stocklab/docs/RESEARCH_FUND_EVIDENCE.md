# Hedge Fund Edges and the "Top-Down Funnel" Hypothesis: An Evidence Review

*(Research agent output, reviewed and adopted. Hypothesis under test: "hedge funds
succeed via global sentiment (inflation/rates) -> field sentiment (supply/demand,
e.g. spotting the chip shortage early) -> picking the stocks best positioned to
solve big problems.")*

**Short answer:** the aggregate data say hedge funds as a class deliver little or
no post-fee alpha; the documented durable winners succeed mostly through
mechanisms that are the *opposite* of a discretionary top-down funnel
(massive-breadth short-horizon aggregation, capacity control, fee-financed
infrastructure, or concentrated bottom-up quality/activism). The macro layer has
the worst evidence of any layer; the field/supply-demand layer is real but
short-horizon and data-intensive; the "world-changing company" layer is, as
commonly practiced, a documented losing strategy at the point it becomes visible.

## 1. Aggregate performance reality

- HFRI Fund Weighted Composite: ~+40% end-2007..mid-2018 vs S&P 500 +148%.
- Database biases: survivorship ~3pp/yr + backfill ~1.4pp/yr (Fung-Hsieh FAJ 2009);
  Malkiel-Saha 2005 finds worse in early samples.
- Ibbotson-Chen-Zhu (FAJ 2011), 13,383 funds 1995-2009: pre-fee 11.13% =
  fees 3.43% + alpha 3.0% + beta 4.7%.
- Dichev-Yu (JFE 2011): DOLLAR-WEIGHTED returns 3-7pp/yr below buy-and-hold
  (capital arrives after performance); "real alpha of hedge fund investors is
  close to zero."
- Bollen et al (FAJ 2021) "End of an Era?": +225% (1997-2007) vs +25% (2008-2016);
  significantly-positive-alpha funds fell ~20%->~10% of universe.
- Luck vs skill: Kosowski-Naik-Teo (JFE 2007) — top fund alphas NOT explained by
  luck; persistence among superior funds only (Jagannathan et al JF 2010). A
  minority (~10-20%) show real skill; the CLASS nets ~zero to investors.

## 2. The exceptions and what structurally separates them

- **RenTec Medallion** (Cornell 2019): ~66% gross/39% net 1988-2018, no losing
  year, negative market beta — massive-breadth SHORT-HORIZON prediction + hard
  capacity cap (~$10B, employees-only, forced distributions). RenTec's outside
  funds: unremarkable.
- **Multi-strat pods** (Citadel/Millennium/DE Shaw/Point72): dominate all-time
  net-gain rankings (top 20 managers: 41% of industry gains on 16.6% of AUM).
  Pods run MARKET-NEUTRAL under stop-outs; macro/factor exposure hedged at the
  center; pass-through fees finance data/talent (Citadel: $12.5B pass-through
  2022-24); capital returned to stay in capacity. Edge = breadth + netting +
  risk discipline + infrastructure — explicitly NOT directional macro/themes.
- **TCI**: ~18%/yr since 2004 via ~9 concentrated high-quality holdings +
  activist engagement — bottom-up quality + governance, skipping the macro layer.
- Common structural themes: capacity control; fee-financed infrastructure;
  either huge breadth at short horizons or concentration WITH an ownership
  lever; ruthless risk management. None of the documented persistent winners
  is described as "macro sentiment -> theme -> story stock."

## 3. Macro timing (funnel layer 1) — weakest evidence of all

- Welch-Goyal (RFS 2008; 2022 update): canonical predictors fail out-of-sample.
- Tactical-allocation funds (the mandate IS macro->allocation): 0 of 12
  survivors matched a 60/40 over the decade to 2023; half the category died.
- Global macro style: flat-to-low returns 2010-2019; 2022 one vintage year.
- Graded public macro forecasters: 47% directional accuracy (~coin toss).
- Fed-cycle sector rotation: great in-sample (Conover 2008), fails out-of-sample
  (Molchanov-Stangl 2024).
- What survives: mechanical, diversified TREND/regime conditioning — positive in
  every decade for a century across 67 markets, standalone net Sharpe ~0.4 (AQR).
  This is REACTING to prices, not forecasting inflation.

## 4. Thematic anticipation (funnel layer 2) — mixed

- REAL when granular+proprietary+short-horizon: satellite parking-lot data gave
  a 4-5% edge in the 3 days around earnings (Katona et al JFQA), accruing to
  feed buyers; edge decays as data spreads (Zhu RFS 2019); alt data is
  informative about SHORT-term outcomes and degrades long-horizon forecasting
  (Dessaint-Foucault-Fresard JF 2024). Industry-concentrated managers do
  outperform (Kacperczyk et al) — domain depth is real, at earnings-cycle
  horizons.
- LOSING in its public "invest in the theme" form: thematic ETFs launch at peak
  excitement and lose ~30% risk-adjusted over 5 years (Ben-David et al RFS
  2023); ARK destroyed ~$14B dollar-weighted over its cycle; IPO/story cohorts
  underperform ~5 years (Loughran-Ritter).
- Chip-shortage case: the theme was TRUE and consensus by mid-2020; the
  profitable versions were (i) short-horizon channel/inventory data around
  earnings, or (ii) holding quality semis THROUGH a -35% drawdown — a
  quality-and-conviction trade, not sentiment detection.

## 5. "Problem-solver" picking (funnel layer 3) — unsupported as stated

- Glamour vs value: extreme glamour 9.3%/yr vs value 19.8%/yr (LSV JF 1994).
- "Big Market Delusion" (Cornell-Damodaran FAJ 2020): in huge-market industries
  EVERY firm gets priced as the winner -> collective overpricing.
- What works: QUALITY/PROFITABILITY at sensible prices (Novy-Marx; QMJ across
  24 countries) — the market underprices boring quality. TCI is exactly this.
- Bessembinder cuts both ways: 4% of stocks = all net wealth creation, but the
  MEDIAN pick loses to T-bills; winners imposed 60-95% interim drawdowns
  (AMZN -94%, NVDA -66%) that dollar-weighted evidence shows holders don't sit
  through.

## 6. What a small/individual system can replicate

(a) PUBLIC DATA, MONTHLY+ HORIZONS (evidence-backed, modest): trend/regime
    conditioning (Sharpe ~0.3-0.4 standalone); quality+value tilts (long-only
    ~0.5-2pp/yr; L/S paper factors 0.3-0.6 Sharpe gross). Haircut everything
    by McLean-Pontiff decay (-26% OOS, -58% post-publication). PEAD is dead in
    large caps since ~2006.
    Fundamental law: IR = IC x sqrt(breadth). 12 independent monthly calls at
    a STRONG IC 0.05 -> IR ~0.17. Factor portfolio, ~100 independent bets/yr
    at IC 0.03 -> IR ~0.3. Honest ceiling: net IR 0.2-0.5.
(b) REAL BUT BUDGET-GATED: alt data (5-7 figures/yr, short-horizon, decaying),
    expert networks, execution/borrow infrastructure, the entire multi-strat
    model.
(c) MOSTLY ILLUSION AT ANY INDIVIDUAL BUDGET: short-horizon directional macro,
    discretionary macro->sector rotation, narrative/theme entry at visibility.

## 7. The evaluation problem

Power comes from INDEPENDENT BETS. 5 years of daily data = ~20 quarterly
thesis-bets (fewer when themes correlate — in 2020-21 "long semis/innovation/
duration" was one rates bet).
- Hit-rate: distinguishing a true 55% caller from a coin needs ~384 quarterly
  bets ~ 96 years.
- Sharpe: t ~ SR x sqrt(years). SR 0.5 needs ~16 years for t~2; SR 0.3 ~44y.
- Plus multiple-testing haircuts (t>=3, deflated Sharpe).
Industry response: mechanism-first, cross-market replication to manufacture
breadth, walk-forward + purged CV, decay-aware sizing, paper-then-small-live
with kill criteria, and pod-level DAILY evaluation precisely because quarterly
granularity is uninformative.

## VERDICT ON THE FUNNEL

| Layer | Grade | Where the real version lives | Data required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Macro sentiment -> sector | UNSUPPORTED as forecasting; MIXED for mechanical trend-reaction (Sharpe ~0.4) | months-years, trend overlays | public prices |
| 2. Field supply/demand anticipation | MIXED: real when granular/proprietary/short-horizon; public theme-form loses ~30%/5y | weeks..~2 quarters, around information events | paid alt data / true domain immersion; decays |
| 3. Problem-solver picking | UNSUPPORTED as stated (glamour loses ~10pp/yr); evidenced alternative: quality-at-reasonable-price, or activist control | multi-year via quality/valuation discipline | public fundamentals (quality tilts) |
| Funnel as "how funds succeed" | UNSUPPORTED — winners use breadth+neutrality+capacity control or quality/activism; funnel-like strategies (TAA, thematic, ARK) have the WORST documented records | — | — |

**Practical synthesis: INVERT the funnel.** Mechanical trend/regime conditioning
instead of macro forecasts; treat field anticipation as an event-horizon problem
affordable only in niches you know at operating depth; replace "world-changer"
screens with quality/profitability-at-reasonable-price. Expect net IR 0.2-0.5,
size for 25-60% decay, and accept that at quarterly bet frequency a track record
is statistically indistinguishable from luck for a decade+ — validation must come
from mechanism, cross-sectional consistency, and multiple-testing-aware tests,
not from the P&L.
