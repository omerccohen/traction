# Desk Note — A Bounce Without a Base (Aug 11, 2026)

**Bottom line.** Calm tape, uptrend intact, still a selection market — four of the six busiest fields are splitting into winners and losers rather than moving together. The week's real signal is in the physical panel: nearly every hard commodity is sharply higher over the last month *and still under water over the last quarter*. That is a bounce off a washout, not a confirmed new up-cycle — and the equity fields riding it, oilfield equipment and the power complex, have run further than the commodity has.

## The backdrop
- **Vol 15.46, 26th percentile — calm** (1 day old). Up slightly from 14.9. Risk-on; the splits below read idiosyncratic, not panic.
- **Trend +0.19, uptrend — input has not refreshed in 41 days.** Same value as last week, one day older. Don't lean on it.
- **Valuation 59th percentile — input is ~3 years stale (1,075 days).** Uninformative. Ignore it.

## Where to look

### 1. The hard-asset bounce that hasn't dug out of its hole
Top attention score is **Oil and Gas Field Machinery** (0.921, 12 names, DISPERSION — names decoupling from each other). The field is **+15.7%/21d**, but *cohesion* (how much the names move together) sits at the **1st percentile**: FET +64% and WHD +35% are doing all of it, HMH flat, SEI −12%. Volume influx is *depressed* — thin participation.

**Physical cross-check — it does not confirm cleanly.** Crude (USO) is +8.3%/21d but still **−8.0%/63d**; energy equities (XLE) +7.4%/+6.6%. The commodity moved roughly half as much as the equipment names. And the same shape is everywhere: copper miners (COPX) **+19.2%/21d but only +1.1%/63d** — implying the two months *before* this bounce were about **−15%**. Uranium (URA) **+10.9%/21d, −21.1%/63d** (prior two months ≈ **−29%**). Metals & mining, gold, nuclear and lithium all trace the same arc. Fifteen of twenty proxies are up on the month; nine are still down on the quarter.

**Research question.** Restocking and short-covering off a June–July washout, or a real turn in demand? Pull: US rig count and E&P capex guidance (equipment orders follow *sustained* crude by quarters, not four weeks); FET/WHD backlog and book-to-bill; copper exchange inventories and smelter treatment charges; uranium **term** contract prices, not spot.

### 2. Electric Utilities: Central — most volatile field in the universe, two weeks running
STRESS again (0.892, 45 names, −3.1%/21d), volatility now at the **100th percentile** (96th last week), cohesion at the **0th** — 45 utilities going 45 directions. GFUZ −21%, FISN −20% against CEG +8%, RNW +11%. Broad Utilities (108 names) shows the same split, dispersion 99.6th percentile.

**Physical cross-check — improved, unresolved.** Last week's note said the "AI needs power" story had no physical tailwind. That partly changed: uranium and nuclear 21-day moves roughly doubled (URA +6.0%→+10.9%, NLR +4.2%→+8.9%). But the quarter-long hole is unchanged at −21% and −18%. One month does not fix that.

**Research question.** Signed data-center power purchase agreements (PPAs) and interconnection-queue additions — contracted megawatts, not press releases. Then GFUZ and FISN: down ~20% against a field down 3% is company news, not a sector.
