## What the axis actually is

**The axis is not "data-center exposure." Almost every name in this field claims it. The axis is the BINDINGNESS of that claim — whether the AI load is a signed, collateralized, minimum-take obligation from a creditworthy counterparty served by assets that already exist and are already funded, versus a pipeline number, an interconnection queue position, a non-binding LOI, or a reactor that has not been licensed.** The market spent the 63-day window repricing *contract quality and duration*, not *theme membership*.

Four pieces of hard evidence, in descending order of how cleanly they separate the field:

**1. The bindingness gradient is now visible in the filings themselves — and it is wide.**
- **Binding, collateralized, priced:** Georgia Power signed a **25-year, 3.2 GW electric service contract with OpenAI** (phased service from 2028, incl. 1 GW flexible demand response), part of **>17 GW of contracted large load** ([SO Q2 call](https://finance.biggo.com/news/US_SO_2026-07-30)). AEP holds **69 GW of contracted load additions through 2030**, of which **45 GW in ERCOT is backed by ~$2 billion of customer-provided collateral** ([AEP Q2](https://finance.biggo.com/news/US_AEP_2026-07-30)); its large-load tariffs oblige customers to pay for **"as much as 80-90% of their contracted demand"** — VERIFIED verbatim in the [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4904/000000490426000059/aep-20260630.htm). Dominion has **12 GW under signed electric service agreements** inside a 53.8 GW contracting funnel ([D Q2](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/dominion-q2-2026-slides-data-centers-surge-offshore-wind-81-done-93CH-4828982)). FirstEnergy: **6.4 GW contracted** against a 25 GW forecast ([FE Q2](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/FE/first-energy-reports-strong-second-quarter-2026-results-reaffirms-g3icpr2if7gi.html)). PPL has a **51%-owned JV (Invitium Energy, LLC)** building generation for PA data centers under long-term ESSAs, plus a Kentucky **"Extremely High Load Factor Tariff"** carrying "long-term contracts, minimum revenue payments and collateral security structures" — VERIFIED in the [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/922224/000092222426000044/ppl-20260630.htm).
- **Non-binding:** Oklo's data-center exposure is **non-binding letters of intent** (Equinix named) plus a Feb-2024 LOI booked as a right-of-first-refusal liability — VERIFIED in the [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849056/000162828026054571/oklo-20260630.htm). Nano Nuclear self-describes as a **"pre-revenue company"** ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1923891/000149315226023071/form10-q.htm)). General Fusion has no commercial product and no commercial fusion plant exists anywhere on earth.

**2. Where the cash flow floats, it got hurt; where it is capacity-contracted, it got paid.** The cleanest negative in the whole field is TransAlta: **Alberta spot power averaged $29/MWh in Q2 2026 vs $40/MWh a year earlier** on oversupply, and adjusted EBITDA fell to **$291M from $349M** ([TransAlta Q2](https://transalta.com/newsroom/transalta-reports-strong-second-quarter-results-and-reaffirms-guidance-2026-07-31)). The cleanest positive: **PJM's 2028/2029 Base Residual Auction cleared the entire RTO at the price cap of $325/MW-day** — VERIFIED verbatim in Constellation's [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1868275/000186827526000104/ceg-20260630.htm). Merchant is not uniformly bad and regulated is not uniformly safe; *which market* is what matters.

**3. A large decoupled bloc is why measured cohesion is at the 0th percentile.** Roughly a third of this "field" is not trading on the axis at all:
- **Pinned to announced cash M&A:** **AES** at **$15.00/share** cash (GIP/BlackRock + EQT + CalPERS + QIA; $10.7B equity / $33.4B EV; shareholders approved 2026-06-26) — [EQT release](https://eqtgroup.com/news/consortium-led-by-global-infrastructure-partners-and-eqt-agrees-to-acquire-aes-2026-03-02). **RNW** signed a **definitive Transaction Agreement on 2026-08-11 at $7.02/share cash** with CPP Investments + founder Sumant Sinha, via UK scheme of arrangement — VERIFIED in the [6-K filed 2026-08-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1848763/000119312526343552/d104914d6k.htm). **TXNM** at **$61.25/share** cash to Blackstone Infrastructure, but the termination date was **extended to 2027-05-31** with close pushed to H1 2027 ([PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/txnm-energy-and-blackstone-infrastructure-extend-merger-agreement-302828825.html)). A stock pinned to a fixed cash price mechanically has near-zero correlation to its sector — three of them in one 45-name field will crush cohesion by itself.
- **Foreign private issuers the coarse label swept in:** **8 of 36 names do not file 10-Qs at all** — RNW (UK plc/India ops), KEP (Korea, state-controlled), TAC (Canada), FTS (Canada), AQN (Canada), BEP (Bermuda LP), BEPC (Canada), plus CEPU (Argentina, not in this list). They file 20-F/40-F/6-K and trade on Korean tariff policy, Alberta spot, and Canadian rate base — not on US AI load.

**4. The 21-day move is dominated by earnings surprise, not positioning — and this is where the ranking most disagrees with the tape.** AEP has arguably the best-collateralized contracted book in the field and **raised** FY guidance by $0.10 to $6.25–$6.55, yet fell ~9% because operating EPS of **$1.36 missed the $1.52 consensus**. Southern **beat by $0.13 and guided to the top of its range** and still fell ~5%. Hawaiian Electric fell on a genuine **32% core-EPS miss ($0.13 vs $0.19)**. Ranking by 21-day price would invert several of these calls; this ranking does not.

**Net:** the split is **contracted-and-funded vs. narrative-and-dilutive**, with a second, mechanical split between **on-axis operators** and a **decoupled M&A/foreign bloc**. The 63-day window shows it most honestly: the speculative reactor/fusion complex (OKLO −40%, NNE −32%, GFUZ −17%) and floating-price merchants (HNRG −19%, NRG −13%, TLN −11%) were the losers, while boring contracted rate base (MGEE +10%, D +9%, OTTR +7%, ES +6%, FE +5%) quietly won.

## 4. Method + caveats

- **Basis.** SEC EDGAR submissions API for all 37 CIKs (36 ranked + CEPU), primary documents downloaded and parsed locally, plus the XBRL `companyconcept` API for cross-company balance-sheet comparability. US names: **Q2 2026 10-Qs, period ended 2026-06-30**, filed 2026-07-24 → 2026-08-10. Foreign private issuers: 6-K/20-F/40-F. Earnings-vs-consensus and deal terms cross-checked against company releases and Q2 coverage, each linked inline.
- **Scoring** = weighted read of (a) *bindingness* of data-center exposure — signed/collateralized/minimum-take vs pipeline vs non-binding LOI; (b) regulated recovery vs merchant price exposure and *which* market; (c) latest quarter vs expectations and guidance direction with its stated reason; (d) balance sheet, dilution and dividend safety; (e) whether the name is on the axis at all.
