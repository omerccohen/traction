## 2. Per-company evidence (contracted/operating vs announced-but-not-delivered)

### 1. CEG — Constellation Energy — 82%
Owns the deepest dispatchable/baseload stack in the group: after completing the **Calpine acquisition on Jan 7, 2026**, the combined fleet is **~55 GW — the largest US generator — with ~22 GW nuclear** (68% of supply) plus ~23 GW gas/geothermal ([Power Magazine](https://www.powermag.com/constellation-completes-acquisition-of-calpine-groups-have-55-gw-of-generation-capacity-2/); Calpine 1/7/26 close VERIFIED in [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1868275/000186827526000104/ceg-20260630.htm) XBRL). **Signed (VERIFIED)** hyperscaler/IG nuclear PPAs: Meta–Clinton **1,121 MW** 20-yr (delivery **begins June 2027** when Illinois ZEC lapses), Microsoft–Crane/TMI-1 **~835 MW** 20-yr (targets 2027 restart; NRC fuel-license amendment approved, FERC transferred interconnection rights per 10-Q), and an **additional 920 MW** of 15–20-yr nuclear PPAs to "investment-grade customers" (deliveries 2029–2032; Q2 buyers incl. Walmart/Comcast per [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/07/constellation-signed-920-megawatts-of-new-power-deals-including-a-walmart-ppa-heres-what-it-means-for-ceg-stock/)), plus a 380 MW CyrusOne gas deal at Freestone. **Separation:** essentially none of the marquee hyperscaler volume is delivering *today* — Clinton starts 2027, Crane must physically restart — so the score rests on owned baseload scale, breadth of signed counterparties, and the strongest balance sheet (senior unsecured **BBB+/Baa1**, VERIFIED 10-Q) to actually fund and execute; it ranks #1 on *probability to deliver at scale*, not on delivering-now. Q2 was a **beat** ($2.55 adj EPS) with guidance **raised to $11.50–$12.50** ([StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/CEG/constellation-reports-second-quarter-2026-rtppfjewi2nw.html)), which is why CEG's 21d (+7.6%) bucked the sector's 63d derating.

### 2. VST — Vistra — 74%
Carries the **largest SIGNED hyperscaler-nuclear book in the group — 3,809 MW — from already-operating plants**, both VERIFIED in the [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1692819/000169281926000019/vistra-20260630.htm): (a) **Meta, 2,609 MW** of 20-yr PJM nuclear PPAs (Perry/Davis-Besse/Beaver Valley incl. uprates) with **delivery commencing late 2026 and full operating volume by YE2027** — the *nearest-term at-scale* start in this group; and (b) **AWS–Comanche Peak, 1,200 MW** 20-yr (delivery **Q4 2027**, ramp to full by 2032). Backed by a ~43.7 GW fleet (6,448 MW nuclear + large gas, growing toward ~50 GW with the Cogentrix gas deal). **Separation:** these are contracted but *not yet at contractual start* (plants sell to grid now; hyperscaler delivery begins late-2026→2027), and the widely-cited **$1B Helix Fund (KKR/NVIDIA/KIA)** is an equity-method *investment vehicle* entered June 2026 — infrastructure financing, NOT delivered MW (VERIFIED 10-Q Note 7). Record Q2 adj EBITDA **$1.767B (+30% YoY)** on a revenue miss ([BigGo](https://finance.biggo.com/news/US_VST_2026-08-07)). Ranks a close #2: leads CEG on raw signed-MW and near-term start, trails on owned-baseload scale, leverage, and balance-sheet cushion.

### 3. TLN — Talen Energy — 67%
The **purest "delivering-now" story**: the revised **AWS PPA of up to 1,920 MW** (doubled from the original 960 MW) runs through 2042, and the **front-of-the-meter transition "occurred in April 2026"** — VERIFIED verbatim in the [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1622536/000162253626000066/tln-20260630.htm); it ramps to full volume by 2032 ([Talen IR](https://ir.talenenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/talen-energy-expands-nuclear-energy-relationship-amazon)). Dispatchable base is Susquehanna nuclear (~2.2 GW) plus the **Freedom and Guernsey gas plants (closed Nov 2025)** and legacy fossil, ~13 GW total. **Separation:** it is genuinely delivering *some* firm power to AWS today, but the volume live now is a fraction of the 1,920 MW headline, and the thesis-critical exposure is **concentrated in one nuclear asset and one counterparty**. Balance sheet is the thinnest of the top tier — **$1.58B total liquidity, $231M unrestricted cash** (VERIFIED 10-Q). High conviction on the single deal; lower diversification and funding cushion cap it below the scaled players.

### 4. NRG — NRG Energy — 44%
Real demand and a big dispatchable gas fleet (~25 GW after the LS Power 18-plant acquisition nearly doubled capacity; **no nuclear**), but its flagship data-center supply is **new-build, not contracted-and-operating**: a **1.2 GW combined-cycle gas plant** under the "Bring Your Own Power" model for a hyperscaler, **COD not until late 2029** ($3.2B project, ~$500M EBITDA target; [investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/nrg-q2-2026-slides-32b-data-center-project-unveiled-amid-earnings-miss-93CH-4835177)). **Separation:** this is an announced future build, years from energizing — the opposite of delivering-now — and the core business is retail (8M customers, VERIFIED [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013871/000101387126000020/nrg-20260630.htm)). Q2 was a **miss** (adj EPS $1.49 vs ~$1.66 est) and the balance sheet is the most stretched: **total liquidity fell to $5.3B from $9.6B**, against **$12.0B unsecured + $3.9B secured notes** (VERIFIED 10-Q) — the -15.9% 21d move is genuinely company-specific (miss + LS Power dilution), not just sector beta.

### 5. OTTR — Otter Tail — 16% (LOW relevance — assessed honestly)
**Does not really belong in this thesis.** OTTR is a diversified holding company whose largest earnings driver is a **PVC-pipe maker** (Plastics ~$130M 2026, normalizing to $45–50M by 2028), alongside a small **regulated** electric utility (Otter Tail Power, ~800 MW) and a manufacturing arm. Its "data-center" exposure is a **regulated large-load *pipeline* of ~1,400 MW, ~35% (~490 MW) tied to a data center** ([Q2 slides](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/otter-tail-q2-2026-slides-2b-utility-buildout-plastics-reset-93CH-4835277)) — that is early-stage *interconnection interest served through rate base*, **not a signed merchant hyperscaler PPA**, and a regulated utility does not sign merchant firm-power PPAs at all. It is included only for completeness; its +6.8% and its raised guidance ($5.68–$6.08) reflect **plastics stabilization and a defensive regulated bid**, decoupled from the AI-power trade — not positioning to deliver contracted firm power to hyperscalers.

## 3. Why the selloff (company-specific vs sector)
- **Sector-wide:** the AI-power trade de-rated over the 63d window (CEG −13.3%, VST −8.7%, TLN −11.0% all with the tape) on valuation and durability-of-demand worries (incl. Texas data-center load "audits" flagged on VST's call).
- **CEG (+7.6% 21d):** company-specific *strength* — Aug-6 Q2 beat + guidance raise bucked the sector.
- **VST / TLN:** mostly sentiment/"sell-the-news"; underlying signed positioning intact.
- **NRG (−15.9% 21d, worst):** company-specific — Q2 EPS **miss**, LS Power interest/dilution drag, liquidity drawdown.
- **OTTR (+6.8%):** decoupled defensive/regulated + plastics stabilization; not a hyperscaler signal.

## 4. Method + caveats
- **Basis:** SEC EDGAR submissions API + each company's **Q2 2026 10-Q** (period ended 2026-06-30) pulled directly, cross-checked with Q2 earnings coverage and deal announcements. Every MW figure is tagged **VERIFIED** (in the 10-Q text/XBRL) or press/company-release-sourced with a link.
- **Scoring** = weighted read of (a) signed hyperscaler PPAs (MW, counterparty, operating vs years-out), (b) owned dispatchable/baseload GW, (c) time-to-energize, (d) balance-sheet strength, (e) real selloff cause. It is a **probability of positioning to deliver**, NOT a price target, forecast, or recommendation.
- **Discriminator applied:** signed-and-(partly)-delivering (TLN April-2026 FOTM; VST Meta late-2026) weighted above signed-but-2029+ (CEG's 920 MW; NRG's 2029 build) above pipeline-interest (OTTR). CEG still ranks #1 because owned-baseload scale + IG balance sheet + breadth of signed deals give the highest probability of delivering *at scale*, even though its marquee volumes start 2027+.
- **LOW-confidence / caveats:** fleet-GW totals and the ~835 MW Crane figure are press/company-sourced (round numbers), not line-item 10-Q disclosures; near-term delivered-MW under each PPA is not separately quantified in filings (ramp schedules are the cited endpoints); OTTR's ~490 MW data-center pipeline is management commentary, not contracted. FERC/NRC and interconnection approvals remain open execution risks for CEG-Crane and all PJM/ERCOT ramps.
