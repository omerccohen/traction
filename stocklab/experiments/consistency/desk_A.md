# Desk Note — 11 Aug 2026

**BOTTOM LINE.** This is a selection market, not a beta market — four of the six busiest fields are splitting into winners and losers rather than moving together, and no regime trigger fired. The one item worth real work is the power complex: utility equities are under pressure while their fuel chain (uranium, nuclear) bounced over a month but sits ~20% lower over three months, so the power-demand story is *not* confirmed on the physical side. Treat the macro backdrop as barely legible — only 2 of 12 indicators carry current data.

## The backdrop (thin)

- **Volatility — calm, and real.** VIX 15.28, 24th percentile, 1 day old. Low-vol tape; moves should be idiosyncratic, not systemic.
- **Trend — uptrend, but six weeks old.** The 12-month S&P trend prints +18.8% and the pack calls it "live," yet it is 42 days stale — early July, not today. A fresh *fetch* is not current *data*.
- **Valuation — ignore it.** PE10 is 1,076 days (~3 years) old, as are CPI and the monthly 10-year.
- **Missing:** 10y yield, 2s10s curve, high-yield credit spreads, breakevens, and all three semiconductor production series — no credit or rates read exists this week.

No triggers, nothing elevated — genuinely benign, no drama to manufacture.

## Where to look

Scores are bunched (0.921 to 0.850 across all six) — don't over-read the ranking. No field is QUIET this week, so nothing needs de-prioritizing on that basis, but the top field's *volume influx* is depressed: price moving without money arriving.

### 1. Power complex — Utilities (0.908) + Electric Utilities: Central (0.892)

One story, not two: the sub-field sits inside the parent, and GFUZ (-21%) tops both mover lists. Electric Utilities: Central is in STRESS — falling with volatility at the 100th percentile. Cohesion (how tightly names move together) is zero in both; Utilities is -3.4% over 21 days, dispersion 99.6th percentile.

**Physical does not confirm.** Uranium (URA +10.9%) and nuclear (NLR +8.9%) rose over 21 days while the equities fell — and over 63 days both are deeply negative (URA -21.1%, NLR -18.2%). A real power-demand cycle shows up in fuel over a quarter, not one month. Solar doesn't back the renewable winners either: TAN -0.8%/21d, -16.4%/63d, against RNW +11%.

**Go check:** interconnection-queue filings and data-center power agreements against uranium *term* prices, not spot. Also verify membership — the biggest Utilities movers, ADTN (-38%) and FN (+11%), look like communications-equipment names, which would make part of this dispersion a taxonomy artifact.

### 2. Oil and Gas Field Machinery (0.921) — top score, thinnest field

Twelve names, huge range: FET +64%, WHD +35%, SEI -12%; field +15.7% over 21 days, volatility 98th percentile. **Physical half-confirms:** crude (USO +8.3%) and energy (XLE +7.4%) rose over 21 days, so direction agrees — but crude is -8.0% over 63 days, and the equipment names are running ~2x the barrel on weak volume. **Go check:** US land rig and frac-spread counts plus completion-equipment backlog; if backlog isn't inflecting, this is order timing, not a capex cycle.

### 3. Food Chains (0.863) — STRESS, with a contradiction inside it

Grocers and convenience are -9.9% over 21 days, dispersion at the 100th percentile: ARKO -46% and ACI -17% against SFM +6%. Yet the universe's second-biggest dislocation is UTZ +84% — a packaged-food *supplier*. Not a commodity story: agriculture (DBA) is flat, +0.2%/21d, -0.5%/63d. **Go check:** grocery same-store sales and gross margin versus supplier price realization — is pricing power moving from the shelf to the brand?
