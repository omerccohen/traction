## Where to look

### 1. Utilities — stressed equities, rallying fuel

Utilities (108 names, 0.908) and Electric Utilities: Central (45 names, 0.892) are one signal counted twice — GFUZ -21% and RNW +11% drive both. The sub-field is STRESS: -3.1% over 21d, cohesion zero, **volatility at the 100th percentile — the most volatile field on the board**.

**Proxy contradiction.** Over the same 21 days URA (uranium) is **+10.9%** and NLR (nuclear) **+8.9%**: the physical fuel is bid while the equities that burn it are stressed. Both proxies are deeply negative over 63d (URA -21.1%, NLR -18.2%), so that's a bounce inside a drawdown, not a new demand regime — and SOXX at **-3.5%** doesn't confirm the datacenter leg either.

**Go check:** rate-case and allowed-ROE rulings; PJM/ERCOT capacity clearing prices and interconnection queues; signed datacenter PPAs vs. announced LOIs. Hygiene flag: the bucket's biggest movers (ADTN -38%, FN +11%) are networking/optical businesses, not power generators — verify the mapping first.

### 2. Oil & Gas Field Machinery — top score, thinnest support

Highest attention (0.921), DISPERSION, but **12 members**, two of which (FET +64%, WHD +35%) carry the +15.7% field move while SEI is -12%.

No backlog means two re-ratings, not a capex cycle.

### 3. Food Chains — stress with no input-cost story

All three are flat-to-negative over 63d (+1.1%, -5.4%, +1.8%), so it's a recent spike — check LME/COMEX inventories for disruption vs. real demand.
